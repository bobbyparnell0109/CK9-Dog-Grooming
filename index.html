About Cathy

A Lifetime of Loving Dogs

Cathy has been grooming dogs since 1993 — not just as a career, but as a true calling. From the very start she had a gift for making even the most nervous or anxious dogs feel calm, safe, and entirely at ease.

She opened CK9 Dog Grooming in 2001 right here in King's Lynn, and over two decades later the ethos hasn't changed one bit. City & Guilds qualified and a competitor at national grooming competitions, Cathy brings professional expertise and genuine warmth to every single appointment.

Whether it's a first puppy trim or a weekly pamper, every dog that walks through our door is treated exactly as Cathy would treat her own.
